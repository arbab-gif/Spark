How filling in an empty My profile field works, using Helpful to know as the example.

  1. Tap the dashed Add something helpful card.
  2. Tap a starter, or just type. The counter keeps it short.
  3. Tap Add. Add up to 3 notes, remove with ×.
  4. Tap Done. The card fills in on your profile.
  5. Tap the note on your profile to edit it again.
